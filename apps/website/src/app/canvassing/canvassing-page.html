Canvassing & turfs

A highlighter on a paper map is not a field plan.

Somebody spends an evening colouring streets, somebody else retypes clipboard notes at midnight, and a volunteer still gets sent across a boundary into doors that cannot vote for you.

pplCRM cuts your list into walkable turfs that stop at your race’s own lines, prints each one as a walk sheet, and puts the same doors on every volunteer’s phone. Every knock comes back live.

Status nobody has to remember to update.

A roster, not a name

A turf holds a whole crew. Each canvasser gets their own personal link — sent automatically by email and text to the contacts on their record — and the turf page shows doors and conversations per canvasser. Removing one person kills only their link.

Refresh the doors, keep the knocks

Cut from a smart list, a turf can re-read it mid-campaign: new movers appear, people who left the list drop off, and every knock already recorded stays exactly where it was.

The printable walk sheet

Paper is a feature, not a fallback.

Some of your best canvassers will never install anything, and phones die at 4 p.m. Every turf prints as a walk sheet: a map with numbered doors, the door list in walking order with blank result columns, and a QR code that scans straight into the same turf on a phone.

Print it mid-campaign

Doors already knocked print with their result, so a paper round never retreads finished ground. The map, the phone and the sheet share one walking order — pin 3 on the map is row 3 on the paper.

The QR code closes the loop

A volunteer handed a sheet at the launch can scan its QR code and carry the same turf on their phone — or stay on paper all day and have someone type the sheet in later. Both are first-class.

It degrades, it doesn’t fail

The sheet’s map is a grayscale street map with the route drawn over it. No Google Maps key configured? It falls back to a schematic with street names and keeps printing.
